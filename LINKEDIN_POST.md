## 🎯 Best Practices for LinkedIn Posts

### Timing:
- **Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM** (best engagement)
- Avoid weekends for professional content

### Format:
- Start with hook (emoji + strong statement)
- Use line breaks (improves readability)
- Include numbers (attracts attention)
- End with question (encourages comments)

### Hashtags:
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Mix popular + niche tags
- Research trending tags in your field

### Engagement:
- Reply to ALL comments in first hour
- Ask follow-up questions
- Share in relevant groups
- Tag connections who'd be interested
